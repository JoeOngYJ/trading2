# BTC spot/perpetual continuation-information plan

Candidate ID: `btc-spot-perp-continuation-information-v1`  
Current stage: **D0 label-blind source qualification**  
Actionable arm: `no_trade`

## Research boundary

This is not another breakout-event catalogue and does not claim to identify a causal news
driver. The eventual falsifiable question is whether two signed spot/perpetual observations add
next-72-hour BTC spot-return information beyond a frozen price-only control. All 2017--2025 BTC
outcomes are consumed development evidence. Existing 2026 history remains sealed or ineligible.

The rejected MCS3-P persistence score may appear only as prior negative evidence. The planned
price terms are controls, not a materially new alpha family. Funding, open interest,
liquidations, news, VECM, Hasbrouck leadership, alternative horizons, event thresholds and model
search are excluded from v1.

## Stages

### R0 — state and mandate reconciliation

- Preserve compression-breakout BEX1 and CUSUM TNE1-A as closed, insufficient-sample evidence.
- Permit public perpetual klines as information inputs only; candidate execution remains spot
  long/flat and is not yet implemented.
- Keep one active candidate identity and zero accepted strategy arms.

### D0 — label-blind source qualification

- Reuse the accepted segmented spot 5-minute ledger and the checksummed 2020--2025 hourly
  perpetual execution archives.
- Use official unauthenticated REST klines only for the pre-2020 warm-up unavailable from the
  monthly archive. Preserve raw responses and require exact overlap with the official
  2019-12-31 daily archive and the 2020 monthly archive.
- Recheck every 2020--2025 monthly sidecar without overwriting frozen source bytes. Any upstream
  revision fails D0 and requires a separately frozen replacement audit.
- Require at least 99% common hourly coverage over 2020--2025 and 98% in every year. Every
  discontinuity creates a segment boundary.
- With a 90-day past-only warm-up, require at least 300 feature-ready daily decisions before
  2021, at least 330 in every 2021--2025 year and at least one in every evaluation month.
- Do not calculate or serialize any future-return label, forecast, strategy return or PnL.

### D1 — separately frozen after D0 passes

One 00:05 UTC decision consumes information completed by 00:00 UTC. The sole target will be the
raw next-72-hour BTC spot log return between exact eligible 5-minute opens. B0 is an expanding
historical mean. M0 contains frozen price-path, realized-volatility and abnormal spot-turnover
controls. M1 adds only a past-standardized 24-hour spot/perpetual basis impulse and separately
past-standardized spot-minus-perpetual abnormal quote turnover.

No D1 label or model may be implemented until its complete formulas, estimator, chronology,
purge, uncertainty method, deterministic seeds, conditional strategy mapping and rejection gates
are frozen under a new contract after D0.
