# Cash-ETF C1 Source Gate Result

Experiment: `cash-etf-c1-prepartition-v1`  
Decision: **C1 blocked before numeric ledger construction**

## Result

The data-only prerequisite passed. It bound 22 existing checksummed normalized sources and wrote
66 development, validation and historical-confirmation files. The copier extracted only each
row's `session` or `action_date` and copied the original bytes. It did not deserialize OHLC,
volume, FX, split factors or dividend amounts and computed no return, feature, forecast, signal,
trade or PnL. No 2026 row was present or permitted.

C1 itself is blocked because the immutable A1 completion review established that complete issuer
corporate-action histories have not been reconciled. Thirteen samples and the BIL split are not
enough to prove the full 2009–2023 total-return ledger. Building returns from the provider rows
would silently weaken the frozen plan and the repository research standard.

The validation and 2024–2025 confirmation files remain numerically unread. Neither frozen C2
strategy may run. No proxy, execution instrument, strategy arm, paper route or live route is
approved.
